IEEE Access-style manuscript: CAFN (cross-attention fusion of 3D MRI and 1D electrophysiology)
=============================================================================================
Build (from this folder):  tectonic -X compile main.tex      (or: pdflatex main.tex, twice)
Regenerate everything from the raw experiment results (from the repo root):
    .venv/bin/python docs/make_results.py && .venv/bin/python docs/build_p2b.py && .venv/bin/python docs/build_paper.py

Files
  main.tex                 assembled manuscript (generated by docs/build_paper.py from parts/*.tex and the v1 head/tail)
  parts/*.tex              section sources (p1 intro-to-setup, p2a engineering, p2b synthetic results [generated],
                           p2c real-cohort results, p3 discussion/conclusion, abstract)
  numbers.tex              measurements of the VEP conditioning chain (original synthetic-VEP verification)
  numbers_results.tex      key numbers of the controlled benchmark, GENERATED from docs/results_raw by build_p2b.py
  tab_*.tex, ../results_assets/   tables and figures generated from the raw results (docs/results_raw)
  figs/                    figures drawn for the paper (fig_arch_v2.png: architecture)
  refs.tex                 hand-written IEEE-style bibliography (39 entries)
  main_v1_backup.tex       the original manuscript (written before any model was trained)
  ieeeaccessapprox.cls     LOCAL APPROXIMATION of the IEEE Access layout

IMPORTANT
- The official ieeeaccess.cls was not available; to submit, paste the body of main.tex into the official
  template (https://ieeeaccess.ieee.org/) and change \documentclass{ieeeaccessapprox} to \documentclass{ieeeaccess}.
- Yellow boxes are placeholders to fill in: author e-mail, funding statement, acknowledgement (guide name), biography.
- Every number in Section V comes from docs/results_raw (copied from the training runs); the prose of Study A is
  generated from those files by docs/build_p2b.py so text and data cannot drift apart.
- Results are from a controlled SYNTHETIC benchmark and from two public real cohorts (depression/MEG, zoster neuralgia/EEG),
  not from glaucoma or optic-neuritis patients. No diagnostic accuracy is claimed.
- References were checked against Crossref, DataCite and publisher pages on 15 Oct 2026 (Dhar et al. author order, pages and DOI corrected; dataset entries now carry year, version and DOI).
  CAMF (Zhou et al.) also has a 2026 journal version (NeuroImage: Reports 6(2), 100338, doi 10.1016/j.ynirp.2026.100338) with different numbers; the arXiv version is cited here.
